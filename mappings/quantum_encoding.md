## 2. Block Encoding Oracle Specifications
An $(\alpha_{\mathcal{A}}, a, \epsilon)$-block encoding embeds $\mathcal{A}$ into an $(n_{state} + a)$-qubit unitary $U_{\mathcal{A}}$:
$$
(\langle 0|^{\otimes a} \otimes \mathbf{I}) U_{\mathcal{A}} (|0\rangle^{\otimes a} \otimes \mathbf{I}) = \frac{\mathcal{A}}{\alpha_{\mathcal{A}}}
$$
- **Subnormalization Constant**: $\alpha_{\mathcal{A}} = \|\mathcal{A}\|_\infty = \max_i \sum_j |\mathcal{A}_{ij}| \approx 7.357$
- **State Qubit Count**: $n_{state} = \lceil \log_2((T_{total} + 1) \cdot D) \rceil$
- **Ancilla Qubit Count**: $n_{ancilla} = \lceil \log_2(s_{max}) \rceil + 2$
- **Condition Number**: $\kappa(\mathcal{A}) = \mathcal{O}(T)$, stabilized by final-state idling (Ueno 2026).
